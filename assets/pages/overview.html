Introduction

Kestrel Integrator Team Created: June 30, 2026

Kestrel Integrator is built around projects, reusable connections, workflows, services, consoles, and a catalog of small runtime utilities. This guide walks you through the product one step at a time.

What this docs site covers
  • Projects, connections, workflows, services, and consoles
  • All built-in utility groups with parameters and usage notes
  • Search-friendly reference pages for quick lookup

Quickstart

Open a project, configure a connection, build a workflow or service, and then use the console or monitor pages to run and inspect your work.
