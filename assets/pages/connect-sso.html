Administration / Connect SSO

Connect an OpenID Connect provider.

Register identity providers for the Kestrel login flow. Multiple providers can be saved, with only one active at a time.

Register a provider

  1. In your identity provider, register an OpenID Connect client and allow the callback URL https://YOUR-KESTREL-HOST/api/auth/sso/callback.
  2. Open Administration → Connect SSO → Register SSO.
  3. Enter provider name, issuer URL, client ID, client secret, scopes, authentication method, and claim names. When issuer discovery is available, leave the optional endpoint URLs blank.
  4. Save and enable the provider when ready. Verify login with a test account; review the resulting account in Users.
Provider name: Corporate Keycloak
Issuer URL: https://sso.example.com/realms/kestrel
Client ID: kestrel-integrator
Client secret: [secret from identity provider]
Scope: openid profile email
Client authentication method: client_secret_basic
Email claim: email
Username claim: preferred_username
Display name claim: name
Redirect URI: https://YOUR-KESTREL-HOST/api/auth/sso/callback

Use the exact public origin and callback path in the identity provider. A mismatched redirect URI, issuer, client secret, or claim mapping can prevent login. A blank secret while editing retains the saved secret. Provider cards offer View, Edit, Enable, and Disable actions.
