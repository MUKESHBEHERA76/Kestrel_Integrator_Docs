Administration / Users

Manage accounts and access.

The Users page separates application accounts from accounts created through OpenID Connect login.

Find and manage accounts

Open Administration → Users. Search by account details, then combine Account status and provider filters. The page shows application authorized users, SSO authorized users, pending approvals, and administrator counts. Export downloads the filtered list as CSV.

A local account can be created with a username, password, and one of three roles: Administrator, Developer, or Viewer. For an SSO account, the user signs in through the configured provider; an administrator reviews any pending approval and assigns the appropriate role. User rows support role and enabled-state changes, approval or rejection where applicable, and deletion with confirmation.

Choose the least privileged role needed. The current user's own status cannot be disabled from their row. An SSO-authenticated administrator has view-only access to user management in the current UI; creation and deletion are unavailable. SSO account controls may be unavailable when SSO is disabled.
